Our Story

“
”

And then, there was us. Two people who were busy becoming who they wanted to be somehow found each other.

We didn't meet with a plan to write a forever. We just started talking, became friends, became each other's people… and somewhere between the conversations, the waiting, the distance and all those little moments, we fell in love. We never asked each other to dream smaller. Instead, we stood beside each other and kept saying, “Go, I’ve got you.” Maybe that's what made us work.

Two different personalities. Two different journeys. Two people deeply passionate about their own lives — and somehow, the right kind of love made space for both. And through every little coincidence, every prayer and every blessing, we kept feeling that Shiva had a little something to do with our story. 🔱

Today, those two separate journeys are becoming one. On *21st November*, with Shiva's blessings, we aren't just getting married. We're choosing to build the rest of our lives together.

He had his dreams. She had hers. And somehow, God made them meet. ❤️
